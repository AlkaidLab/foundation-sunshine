MAT capability negotiation and fallback — 2026-10-22
==================================================
Scope: session-start output/host selection for the existing finite MAT playback
contract. Unsupported phones do not need a MAT decoder or object renderer.

Implemented
-----------
Normal MAT preference checks the current media output before native startup.
Explicit MAT advertisement, verified HDMI/ARC/eARC route, direct IEC transport,
bounded minimum buffer and a zero-byte track on that actual device are required.
Unverifiable output selects Opus, retaining the user's preference for a later
reconnect. The native AUTO preference 6 never reaches the wire: a compatible
DESCRIBE selects codec 5, otherwise codec 0 is announced without MAT attributes.

Strict diagnostic codec 5 and dump codec 4 retain their rejection behavior.
MAT confirmation mismatch is terminal; stale MAT confirmation after Opus
selection is also rejected. Established MAT output pins its device and fails
permanently on unknown, changed or non-HDMI routing, including route callbacks.
The real audio startup queue, initial sequence and frame duration now depend
on the final negotiated codec, including AUTO selecting MAT or Opus.
